FP16 TP=2 and W8A16 TP=2 use the same prompts, seeds, sampling, limits, warmups, and repeats. With `--enforce-eager`, both modes are eager; W8A16 is always eager-only, while FP16 may use CUDA Graph under the default policy. Therefore default-policy results must not be interpreted as isolating quantization kernel cost.
